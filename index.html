How it works

Every topic page follows the same three steps.

  1. Read the lesson

    A short explanation of the idea and why DevOps teams use it, with the key points to remember.

  2. Practise in the terminal

    Type real commands (git, docker, kubectl, terraform…) into a practice terminal and see what they return.

  3. Pass the quiz

    Get all three questions right to load the container, earn 100 XP and unlock the next topics.
